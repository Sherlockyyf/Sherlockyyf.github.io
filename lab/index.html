About the Lab实验室简介

MUSE Lab stands for Multimodal Understanding and Synthesis for Explainability. The Muses were the Greek goddesses of art and inspiration: we want generative AI to be just as creative, and at the same time controllable, interpretable and trustworthy.

MUSE Lab 的全称是 Multimodal Understanding and Synthesis for Explainability，即“面向可解释性的多模态理解与生成”。MUSE 也是希腊神话中司掌艺术与灵感的缪斯女神：我们希望生成式 AI 像缪斯一样富有创造力，同时可控、可解释、值得信任。

Featured Work代表作

Concept Brittleness

Asked for an ink painting, why does an image generator paint grapes in black-and-white ink but always paint a cake in color? We found what makes the result depend on the object being drawn, and fix this “object-level concept brittleness” without any training.

同样画一幅水墨画，为什么图像生成模型能把葡萄画成黑白水墨色，而却把蛋糕始终画成彩色的？我们找到了这种因为画的物体不同而产生差异的原因，无需训练即可修正这种“物体级的概念脆弱性”。

Semantic Discrepancy

Turning a photo into a painting, diffusion models invent things that were never there. One inversion step fixes it.

照片转油画时，模型会凭空“脑补”原图里没有的东西。一步反演就能修正。

Learn more了解更多Code

SurveyGraph

An AI that first maps how a research field evolved, then writes a fully cited survey along that map.

让 AI 先画出一个领域的“方法—实体”知识图谱，再沿着它写出一篇有据可查的学术综述。

Learn more了解更多Code

StateTrack

LLMs still remember the rules you set; deep into a conversation they quietly stop following them. We found this collapse and measured it.

大模型还记得你定下的规则，却在长对话里悄悄不再遵守。我们发现并量化了这种“状态崩塌”。

Learn more了解更多Code

ReMCTS

Code-writing agents try many programs in a search tree, and every failed branch teaches the others.

写代码的智能体在一棵搜索树上多路尝试，失败分支的经验会分享给其他分支，少走弯路。
