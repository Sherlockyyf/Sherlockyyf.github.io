Building trustworthy generative AI 构建可信的生成式 AI

I am an Assistant Professor at the College of Artificial Intelligence, Shenzhen University, where I lead MUSE Lab (Multimodal Understanding and Synthesis for Explainability). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Fudan University in 2025, advised by Prof. Junping Zhang, and my B.S. in Physics from Fudan University in 2019. I am a member of the Technical Committee on Hybrid Intelligence and the Science Popularization Committee of the Chinese Association of Automation (CAA).

我是深圳大学人工智能学院助理教授、硕士生导师，负责 MUSE Lab（Multimodal Understanding and Synthesis for Explainability Lab）。2025 年博士毕业于复旦大学计算机科学技术专业，师从张军平教授；2019 年本科毕业于复旦大学物理学专业。现为中国自动化学会混合智能专委会、普及工作专委会会员。

My research aims to make generative AI controllable, interpretable, and trustworthy. I study why text-to-image diffusion models succeed or fail on specific concepts and how to edit images and videos precisely and consistently; how large language models and agents stay reliable — free of hallucination, bias, and state collapse — over long-horizon interaction; and how generative agents can perceive and express human emotion. I am also interested in bringing these models to real-world applications such as emotional companionship, virtual try-on, and digital humans.

我的研究目标是让生成式 AI 更加可控、可解释、可信：研究文生图扩散模型为何在特定概念上成功或失败，以及如何实现精确、一致的图像与视频编辑；研究大语言模型与智能体如何在长程交互中保持可靠，避免幻觉、偏见与状态崩塌；研究生成式智能体如何感知并表达人类情感。同时探索这些技术在情感陪伴、虚拟试衣、数字人等真实场景中的落地应用。

Interpreting Object-Dependent Concept Brittleness in Text-to-Image Diffusion Models
Yifan Yuan*, Xiangyu Liu*, Hongming Shan, Yu Han, Yu Jiang, Hao Tan, Junping Zhang, Linlin Shen†

Text-to-image models apply a concept (e.g., “ink sketch”) correctly to most objects yet repeatedly fail on a few. We diagnose this brittleness with step-wise sparse autoencoders on denoising features and repair it at inference time without retraining, across five diffusion backbones.

文生图模型能把某一概念（如“水墨素描”）正确施加到大多数物体上，却在少数物体上反复失败。我们用逐时间步的稀疏自编码器诊断这种“物体依赖的概念脆弱性”，并在推理阶段免训练修复，在五种扩散模型上验证有效。

ACM MM 2026CCF A[Paper][Code]
When LLMs Forget What They Believe: Characterizing State Integrity Collapse in Long-Horizon Dialogues
Yancheng Zhu, Yifan Yuan†

LLMs can repeat an instruction yet stop obeying it. We define State Integrity Collapse and build StateTrack-Bench: across seven models, 88.6% of failures are behavioral drift rather than forgetting, and Structured State Anchoring recovers up to 24.4 points.

大模型能复述指令，却不再遵守它。我们定义了“状态完整性崩塌”并构建 StateTrack-Bench：在七个模型上，88.6% 的失败源于行为漂移而非遗忘；结构化状态锚定最多可挽回 24.4 个百分点。

EMNLP 2026[Paper][Code]
ReMCTS: Reflection-Enhanced Monte Carlo Tree Search for Code Generation
Wang Huifei, Huang Xinying, Sun Yiheng, Yifan Yuan†

An execution-grounded, memory-augmented tree search for code generation that keeps branch-local debugging context and reuses failure experience across branches; it beats direct generation in 8 of 10 model–dataset pairs on HumanEval and MBPP.

面向代码生成的执行驱动、记忆增强的树搜索框架：保留分支内的调试上下文，并跨分支复用失败经验；在 HumanEval 与 MBPP 的 10 组模型—数据集组合中有 8 组优于直接生成。

EMNLP 2026[Paper][Code]
SurveyGraph: Generating Academic Surveys via Methods-Entity Knowledge Graphs
Yifan Yuan, Yancheng Zhu, Zuoyu Zhang, Xiaogang Peng†

SurveyGraph builds a Methods-Entity Knowledge Graph that records how methods extend, compete with, and share resources with one another, and uses it for core-paper discovery, graph-aware retrieval, outline generation, and evolution-aware survey writing.

SurveyGraph 构建方法—实体知识图谱（MEKG），刻画方法之间的继承、竞争与资源共享关系，并据此完成核心论文发现、图感知检索、大纲生成与演化式综述写作。

EMNLP 2026[Paper][Code]
Dissecting and Mitigating Semantic Discrepancy in Stable Diffusion for Image-to-Image Translation
Yifan Yuan, Guanqun Yang, James Z. Wang, Hui Zhang, Hongming Shan, Feiyue Wang†, Junping Zhang

Adding noise to the reference image, as SDEdit does, makes text-to-image models invent content and relations that are not in the original. We trace this “semantic discrepancy” to correlations disrupted during U-Net upsampling and fix it training-free with features from a single DDIM-inversion step, 11.2× faster than PnP.

像 SDEdit 那样直接给参考图加噪，会让文生图模型凭空生成原图中没有的内容与关系。我们发现这种“语义偏差”源于 U-Net 上采样时被破坏的特征相关性，并用单步 DDIM 反演的特征进行免训练修正，速度比 PnP 快 11.2 倍。
